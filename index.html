Polan Riley

My name is Polan Riley and this is my personal webpage. From a young age, I have been driven by curiosity, constantly seeking to understand the depths of various subjects. My technology journey began in fourth grade when I learned to make websites on the old IDE, Mozilla Thimble. It continued in the sixth grade, where I learned to program NXT robots, joined a robotics club, and participated in competitive after-school events. These experiences ignited my passion for technology and solidified my desire to pursue a career in the field. Despite the challenge of choosing a specific path within technology, my love for problem-solving and my eagerness to test my capabilities have always guided me.
